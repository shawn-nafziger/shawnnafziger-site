How I work

01

Lead with the answer

Every title states the finding, not the topic. "Second chances created the separation," not "Rebounding by team."

02

Every number gets context

A rank comes with its count, a value with its normal and its record, and every figure with an "as of" time.

03

Honest about uncertainty

Sample sizes are stated, and a change too small to trust is labeled as noise, not dressed up as a trend.
